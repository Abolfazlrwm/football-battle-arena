# Support

Thanks for your interest in the arena! Here's where to go:

- **Bug or clear feature request** — [open an issue](https://github.com/Abolfazlrwm/football-battle-arena/issues/new/choose)
  using the matching template.
- **Question, idea, or feedback** — [start a discussion](https://github.com/Abolfazlrwm/football-battle-arena/discussions)
  instead of filing an issue.
- **Security vulnerabilities** — please don't open a public issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private disclosure.

This is a solo-maintained frontend showcase, so responses may take a few
days — data discrepancies (captaincies, kits, stats) are worth reporting
with a source, and they get fixed fastest that way.
